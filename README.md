# Wisp Local Agent

Installers for the Wisp desktop app, which lets your Wisps work with files and websites on your own computer.

Download it from inside Wisp at [wispeditor.com](https://wispeditor.com): add a step that runs on your computer, and Wisp shows the installer for your system. The installers are also listed under [Releases](../../releases).

The app isn't code-signed yet, so the first time you open it, macOS will say it couldn't verify the app. Click "Done", then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, click "Open Anyway" next to Wisp Local Agent and confirm.

This repository holds release files only.
